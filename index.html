    ATS tips

    Make your resume easier for hiring systems to read.

    Use exact role keywords Mirror important tools, skills, and job titles from the description when they truthfully match your experience.
    Keep formatting simple Use clear headings and avoid tables, photos, columns, icons, and text boxes in the ATS version.
    Prove impact with numbers Add metrics such as users, revenue, speed, cost savings, accuracy, volume, or percentage improvement.
